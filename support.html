Support

Get help with pairing, permissions, and a missing video picture.

Last updated October 2, 2026. 阅读中文版

Email mirroview@liljackson.org for support or privacy questions.

Start a session

  1. Use the same reachable local Wi-Fi network, or update both phones to beta build 50 or newer to try nearby sharing. Keep Wi-Fi enabled. Screen sharing supports iOS 17 or newer on the sending phone. Internet access is not required for the video session.
  2. On the sending phone, choose to share. The main share button starts an entire-screen session.
  3. On the viewing phone, choose to watch and scan the invitation QR code.
  4. Check the invitation and confirm on both phones. The sender must also approve the iOS screen capture prompt.

After screen sharing starts, the sender can open Camera or another app. The viewer sees the screen, subject to iOS capture restrictions.

Export diagnostics

From beta build 50, tap the version number six times, choose Export logs ZIP, and use the system share sheet. Export from both phones after a failure. Include the approximate time and whether each phone was connected to a router. Do not post diagnostic files publicly.

If the phones cannot connect

  • Check that both phones use the same Wi-Fi and that the network allows devices to communicate with each other. Guest or isolated networks may block this.
  • Allow Local Network access for mirroview in iOS Settings.
  • Start a fresh session and scan the new invitation. Old invitations expire.
  • Keep both apps open during pairing and try again if the network changes.

If the viewer sees no picture

  • Confirm the session on both phones and approve the iOS screen share prompt on the sending phone.
  • Check that the sender has not paused or stopped sharing.
  • Try showing an ordinary app screen. Some protected content cannot be captured.
  • End the session and start a new one if the connection drops.

Audio is not part of the current app. A viewer may still capture a copy of visible video through their device.

Write to us

Include the app version, iPhone models, iOS versions, what you were trying to share, and the steps that led to the problem. Tell us whether pairing failed or video was blank after both people confirmed. Do not email invitation QR codes, passwords, private screen images, or diagnostic archives unless you intend to share them.

开始会话

  1. 让两台 iPhone 接入同一可互访的本地 Wi-Fi，或将两台都更新到测试版本 50 及以上以尝试附近分享。保留 Wi-Fi 开关。整屏发送支持发送方使用 iOS 17 或更高版本，视频会话不要求互联网。
  2. 发送方选择分享。首页的分享按钮默认开始整屏共享。
  3. 观看方选择观看，并扫描邀请二维码。
  4. 双方核对邀请并确认。发送方还要批准 iOS 的屏幕共享授权。

整屏共享开始后，发送方可打开相机或其他 App。观看方看到的是屏幕画面，具体内容受 iOS 的采集限制。

导出诊断

从测试版本 50 开始，连续点击版本号六次，选择“导出日志 ZIP”，再通过系统分享界面保存。失败后请从两台分别导出，并附大致时间及各自是否连接路由器。请勿公开发布诊断文件。

无法连接

  • 确认两台设备使用同一 Wi-Fi，且网络允许设备相互访问。访客网络或隔离网络可能阻止连接。
  • 在 iOS 设置中允许 mirroview 访问本地网络。
  • 重新开始会话并扫描新邀请。旧邀请会过期。
  • 配对时保持两个 App 打开。网络变化后请重试。

观看方没有画面

  • 确认双方都同意，并检查发送方是否已批准 iOS 整屏共享授权。
  • 检查发送方是否暂停或结束了分享。
  • 尝试分享普通 App 画面。部分受保护内容无法被捕获。
  • 如果连接中断，结束后重新开始会话。

当前 App 不传输声音。观看方仍可能通过设备保存可见画面。

反馈问题

请提供 App 版本、两台 iPhone 的型号和 iOS 版本、您想分享的内容，以及复现步骤。请说明是配对失败，还是双方确认后画面仍为空白。除非您明确愿意分享，否则请勿发送邀请二维码、密码、私人屏幕内容或诊断压缩包。
