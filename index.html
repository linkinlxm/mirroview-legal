A shared view, by choice

See the same moment on two iPhones.

Share your iPhone screen with someone nearby. Both people confirm the session, and the sender approves screen capture through iOS.

How it worksRead our privacy policy

Made for a local connection

Pair in person

One person shares an invitation. The other scans it, and both confirm the session.

Show your screen

The home screen starts an entire-screen share. After iOS authorization, the sender can open another app, including Camera, to show its screen.

Stay in control

End the session from either device. The current release requires both devices on the same reachable local Wi-Fi network.

Know before you share

Screen sharing can reveal notifications and other private information. The app does not transmit audio. A viewer may still take a screenshot, record their screen, or photograph what they see. Some protected content may appear blank.

中文说明

两台 iPhone，一起看见同一画面。

mirroview 可在同一可互访的本地 Wi-Fi 网络中分享 iPhone 整个屏幕。双方确认本次连接后，发送方仍需通过 iOS 系统界面授权整屏共享。授权后，发送方可以打开其他 App，包括相机 App，展示其屏幕。当前版本不传输声音，任一方都可以结束会话。

接收方可以截图、录屏或用其他设备拍摄。部分受保护内容可能无法显示。
