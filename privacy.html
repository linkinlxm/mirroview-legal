Privacy policy

This policy describes the current mirroview iOS release and this website.

Effective and last updated October 2, 2026. 阅读中文版

Who to contact

mirroview is an independent iOS app. For privacy questions, email mirroview@liljackson.org.

What the app does

The app uses authenticated local discovery and signaling. A reachable shared Wi-Fi network uses WebRTC video. Beta build 50 supports an authenticated nearby H.264 route for screen sharing on iOS 17 or newer after nearby admission. Connection quality and system support still determine availability. Both people must confirm each session. An entire-screen share also requires the sender's authorization through the iOS system interface. The app does not transmit audio.

Live video is sent to the connected viewer. We do not run an account service or an operator video server for the current release, and the app does not upload session video to us. A viewer can still save or externally photograph what appears on their device.

Permissions and local data

  • Local Network: Finds and connects to the other device on your local network.
  • Camera: Scans an invitation QR code when you choose to scan. Watching a screen does not require camera access.
  • Screen capture: iOS asks the sender to authorize an entire-screen share. The app cannot silently grant this permission. The sender can then open other apps, including Camera, to show their screens.
  • Device orientation: The app uses captured-frame orientation metadata to display shared content.

Short-lived invitations and connection data are used for a session. On older supported iOS versions, the app temporarily places an invitation and status files in a shared app container so its screen broadcast extension can start. The extension consumes the invitation file. The app does not use these files as a video archive.

Storage and diagnostics

The app does not create an account or save session photos and videos. From beta build 50, it keeps bounded diagnostic events on the device, including app and iOS versions, device model, connection stages, numeric errors, and media counters. The records exclude invitation QR codes, keys, IP addresses, Wi-Fi names, full SDP/ICE, and media. A random identifier is used for each process launch; it is not a persistent device identifier. Logs rotate locally. You may export a ZIP through the system share sheet, and only the latest three exports are retained in the app cache. The app does not automatically upload diagnostics. Send an export to support only if you want us to review it. Deleting the app removes its local diagnostic files; files you exported elsewhere remain under your control.

Website and email

This site has no analytics, advertising, trackers, forms, or third-party scripts. Its hosting provider may process ordinary access data needed to serve the pages. If you email us, you choose what to include. We and our email provider process your address and message to answer you and keep support correspondence. To ask about or request deletion of an email you sent, contact the same address.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app or site changes. The date above identifies the latest version.

App 如何工作

App 通过已认证的本地发现和信令建立连接。可互访的共同 Wi-Fi 网络使用 WebRTC 视频。测试版本 50 在附近准入后支持已认证的 H.264 通道，用于 iOS 17 及以上的整屏分享。连接质量和系统支持仍决定功能是否可用。每次会话都需要双方确认。分享整个屏幕时，发送方还须在 iOS 系统界面授权。App 不传输声音。

实时画面会发送给已连接的观看方。当前发布版本没有账户服务或由我们运营的视频服务器，App 不向我们上传会话画面。但观看方仍可保存屏幕内容或用其他设备拍摄。

权限与本地数据

  • 本地网络：发现并连接同一网络中的另一台设备。
  • 相机：仅在您主动扫码时用于读取邀请二维码。仅观看屏幕不需要相机权限。
  • 屏幕录制：发送方需通过 iOS 系统界面授权整屏共享。App 不能静默授予此权限。随后发送方可打开其他 App，包括相机 App，展示其屏幕。
  • 设备方向：App 根据采集帧的方向信息显示共享内容。

短期邀请和连接信息用于当前会话。在较旧的受支持 iOS 版本中，App 会暂时把邀请和状态文件放入共享的 App 容器，以启动屏幕广播扩展。扩展读取后会移除邀请文件。这些文件不是视频存档。

存储与诊断

App 不创建账户，也不在 App 内保存会话照片或视频。从测试版本 50 开始，App 在本机保存有容量限制的诊断事件，包括 App 与 iOS 版本、设备型号、连接阶段、数值错误和媒体计数。不记录邀请二维码、密钥、IP、Wi-Fi 名称、完整 SDP/ICE 或媒体。每次进程启动使用随机标识，不使用持久设备标识。日志在本机轮转。您可通过系统分享界面主动导出 ZIP，App 缓存仅保留最近三个导出文件，不会自动上传诊断。仅在希望我们查看时向支持邮箱发送。删除 App 会移除其本机诊断文件，已导出到其他位置的文件仍由您控制。

网站与邮件

本网站没有分析、广告、追踪器、表单或第三方脚本。托管服务提供方可能处理提供网页所需的普通访问数据。若您发邮件给我们，您自行决定包含哪些信息。我们和邮件服务提供方会处理您的邮箱地址和邮件内容，以回复并保留支持往来。您可发邮件查询或请求删除自己发送的邮件。
